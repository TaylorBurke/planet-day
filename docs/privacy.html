Privacy Policy

Planet of the Day for Apple Watch · Effective November 1, 2026

Planet of the Day does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The app and its watch complication work entirely on your Apple Watch. They read only the current date from your device to choose which planetary symbol to show. They do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, location, health data, or network connections, and no information ever leaves your device.

Planet of the Day

An Apple Watch complication showing each weekday's classical planet.

☉︎ ☽︎ ♂︎ ☿︎ ♃︎ ♀︎ ♄︎

Adding the complication

  1. Touch and hold your watch face, then tap Edit.
  2. Swipe to the complications screen and tap a slot.
  3. Choose Planet of the Day.
